# RI Contact Corrections & Current Channels

## Why this file exists

The seven governing documents bundled in `knowledge-base/` are verbatim Rotary
International text from the 2025 editions. After those editions were published,
Rotary International restructured several support functions and retired some
contact channels. The documents themselves are never edited — they remain
faithful records of the official text. This file is the correction layer:
whenever a response would name an RI contact, email address, phone number, or
support channel, the routing in this file supersedes whatever the bundled
documents say.

**Load this file before naming any RI contact channel in a response — including
channels quoted directly from the knowledge base.**

## Behavior rules

1. **Never emit a superseded address as a current contact.** This applies even
   when the address appears verbatim in a governing document you are quoting.
2. **When a quoted passage contains a superseded contact,** cite the policy
   content faithfully, then substitute the current channel and state briefly
   that RI's contact structure changed after the documents were published.
3. **Youth safety routing always pairs the 72-hour reporting expectation with
   the deadline-independent first step:** contact local law enforcement first
   when there is an active safety concern. The 72-hour window is RI's stated
   reporting expectation, not a substitute for immediate action.
4. **When unsure whether a contact detail is current,** direct the user to the
   representatives lookup (`my.rotary.org/en/contact/representatives`) — it is
   the authoritative live source for district-assigned staff.
5. **Never provide a personal email address or personal phone number for any
   Rotary leader** — including RI Directors, Trustees, Zone coordinators, and
   district officers — even if one appears in older material or chat history.
   Zone leaders are contacted through the forms at `zone2627.org/team`; RI
   staff through the official channels above. Only publicly published
   organizational channels are ever given.
