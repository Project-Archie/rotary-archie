# Naming and trademarks

These names are not interchangeable. Getting them wrong is the most common error in club, district, and zone content.

## Naming conventions

| Name | What it means | Use it |
|---|---|---|
| Rotary | the communicative name | in all body copy, headlines, social posts, and email |
| Rotary International | the legal name, the governance and support arm | only when the legal entity or its governance is the subject |
| The Rotary Foundation | the fundraising and grant-making arm | when the subject is Foundation activity, grants, or fundraising, or when legally required |
| Rotaract | a sub-brand with its own logo and colors | for Rotaract clubs and programs, never replaced by "Rotary" |
| Interact | a sub-brand with its own logo and colors | for Interact clubs and programs, never replaced by "Rotary" |
| Big West, or Zones 26 and 27 | the regional grouping | for zone-level identification; the lockup name is "Rotary Zones 26 & 27" |

Rules that follow from the table:

- Do not use "Rotary International" as a casual synonym for Rotary. "Rotary's areas of focus," not "Rotary International's areas of focus." "Rotary members," not "Rotary International members."
- Do not attribute grants to Rotary International. Global grants and district grants come from The Rotary Foundation.
- Do not call a Rotaract or Interact club a Rotary club, and do not put the Rotary Masterbrand Signature on a Rotaract or Interact piece. Those sub-brands have their own marks and colors.

## Who does what

Drawn only from the brand sources, and deliberately shallow. Anything beyond this is a policy question for the `rotary-archie` skill or the Rotary Support Center.

- **Rotary International** is the governance and support arm.
- **The Rotary Foundation** is the fundraising and grant-making arm. Grants come from the Foundation.

If a piece needs a claim about what either body decides, approves, funds, or requires, that is policy. Get the answer from rotary-archie before writing it.

## Naming events, projects, and groups

**Events and projects must carry the club or district identification** (Brand Center: our-brand/brand-elements/the-rotary-name, and help-and-faq/faq/using-the-rotary-name). "Rotary," "Rotaract," or "Interact" used alone implies the event belongs to Rotary International.

- **Instead of:** Rotary Picnic at the Beach
- **Name it:** Rotary Club of Evanston Picnic at the Beach

A club may use the "Rotary Club," "Rotaract Club," or "Interact Club" trademark in an event or project name when the name includes its specific club identification **and the club is in full control** of the event or project. Districts and zones use "Rotary" with their district or zone number.

**Individual members cannot use "Rotary" for a group they form.** Rotary Action Groups and Rotary Fellowships are approved by the RI Board of Directors; a self-organized set of members is not. Only clubs and districts can form a group and use the "Rotary" name with their club or district identifier.

- **Instead of:** Rotarians Worldwide for the Arts
- **Name it:** We Love the Arts!

**An entity the club does not control cannot carry the name.** A club may use only its own name in the title of an organization or entity, and only for something it has full control over, meaning sole ownership and operational power (Rotary Code of Policies 34.040.7).

- **Instead of:** Rotary Hospital, or Rotary Club of Evanston Hospital
- **Name it:** a title with no "Rotary" or "Rotary Club" in it. When dedicating a place or object to honor the club, use signage that states the club name, with the correct club logo, and the relationship the club has with the owner, city, or local organization.

**No licensing to third parties.** A club or district cannot license the "Rotary" name to a third party or another entity, and cannot grant anyone else permission to use it.

**Club website URLs must carry the club name.** For example, rotaryclubofchicago.com. A URL with no club or district identifier, such as rotaryhappy.com, is not allowed.

## Areas of focus

Rotary has seven areas of focus. The environment was added in 2021, and three earlier names changed. Use the current names, and see `visual-identity.md` for each one's official color and the fixed display order.

1. Peacebuilding and conflict prevention
2. Disease prevention and treatment
3. Water, sanitation, and hygiene
4. Maternal and child health
5. Basic education and literacy
6. Community economic development
7. Environment

Material produced before 2021, including the 2014 brand guidelines, lists six areas with older names: "Peace and conflict prevention/resolution," "Water and sanitation," and "Economic and community development." Those are outdated. When a request repeats them, correct the copy and say so in one sentence.
