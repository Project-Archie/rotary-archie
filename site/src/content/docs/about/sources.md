Archie answers from eight governing documents that Rotary International (RI) publishes on My Rotary. The text inside Archie is RI's own wording, copied from RI's PDF files without paraphrasing. Each title below links to RI's copy, so you can read any document at its source. The one exception is noted in the table.

## The documents

| --- | --- |
| [Bylaws of Rotary International](https://my.rotary.org/en/document/bylaws-rotary-international) | June 2025 |
| [Constitution of Rotary International](https://my.rotary.org/en/document/constitution-rotary-international) | June 2025 |
| [Rotary Code of Policies](https://my.rotary.org/en/document/rotary-code-policies) | January 2027 (Board decisions through October 2026) |

Archie also carries two short RI guidance documents on artificial intelligence, the AI Guidelines for Members and the Member FAQ on AI Use, both dated February 2026.

RI lists all of its governance documents on the [governance documents page](https://my.rotary.org/en/knowledge-and-resources/resources-and-reference/governance-documents) of My Rotary.

## How often RI changes them

RI revises these documents on different schedules.

- The Constitution, the Bylaws, the Standard Rotary Club Constitution, and the Manual of Procedure change every three years, after the Council on Legislation. The Council last met in 2025 and meets next in 2028.
- The Rotary Code of Policies is revised after each RI Board meeting, about four times a year. The Rotary Foundation Code of Policies is revised after each Trustees meeting.
- The District Planning Guide is a training publication that RI updates on its own schedule. Lead Your District: Governor was too, but RI no longer posts it; governor training now runs through courses in the Rotary Learning Center. Archie keeps the last published edition for reference.

## How Archie keeps up

Once a month, the project checks My Rotary for a new edition of every document in the table. When RI publishes one, the project adds the new edition, checks it against RI's list of amendments, and tests Archie's answers. A new version of Archie is then published on [GitHub](https://github.com/Project-Archie/rotary-archie/releases/latest), and the [changelog](/rotary-archie/about/changelog/) says what changed.

Between an RI release and the next Archie release, Archie can be one edition behind. If a decision depends on a recent Board or Trustees action, read the document at its source.

## What the version number means

Archie's version has three numbers, for example 2026.1.4.

- The first number is the Rotary year of the documents. 2026 means the Rotary year that runs July 2026 to June 2027.
- The second number changes when one of the seven core rules Archie follows changes.
- The third number changes for everything else, including a new edition of a Code of Policies during the year.

To see which version you have, ask Archie "What version of Archie are you?" The steps are on [Check your version and update](/rotary-archie/get/update/).
