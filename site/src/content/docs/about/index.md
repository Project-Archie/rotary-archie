## What Archie is

Archie is an AI-powered companion built specifically for Rotary District Governors and their leadership teams. It turns Rotary International's governing documents into a structured, searchable, cited reference. Rather than spending hours searching through the RI Constitution, Bylaws, Code of Policies, Manual of Procedure, and other documents, you can ask Archie a question in plain language and receive a structured answer in seconds, complete with specific section references.

The name was inspired by *archi*, a Sanskrit word meaning illumination, a ray or flame of light. The metaphor reflects what Archie is built to do: light the way for district leaders by making policy easier to find, easier to interpret, and easier to apply with confidence. Whether you need to double-check your reading of a Bylaw, develop an idea for your district year, or work through a sensitive question, Archie is designed to help you see the path more clearly.

## Where Archie came from

Archie was originally developed for Zones 26 and 27's 2025 Education Week in Albuquerque. That event focuses on preparing the incoming District Governor line, specifically District Governors-elect and District Governor-nominees, to be fully equipped for their governor year. The goal was to create a 24/7 resource that governors could rely on for guidance, helping them interpret policy, navigate governance questions, and work through challenges in real time.

During Education Week it became clear that the broader district leadership team, including executive secretaries, committee chairs, and other district leaders, would also benefit from access. Archie evolved from a governor-only tool into a leadership support resource for anyone operating at the district level.

## A community project

Archie is a community project and not an official Rotary International product. Rotary International has not endorsed or adopted it. Archie answers from governing documents that Rotary International publishes on My Rotary, copied without changes to their wording. [Where Archie's documents come from](/rotary-archie/about/sources/) lists each one with its edition and a link to Rotary International's copy, and explains how the project checks for new editions.

Those safeguards are built into Archie's programming. It does not provide legal, financial, or insurance advice. It directs users to RI staff or appropriate leadership channels when escalation is required. It reinforces compliance with official RI policy throughout. And it encourages verification through proper RI or district channels for sensitive matters. These guardrails make Archie a research and reference tool, not a decision-making authority.

## What Archie does

Archie operates in seven modes depending on what you need.

| Mode | What it handles |
| --- | --- |
| **Advisor** | Policy interpretation, compliance checking, governance questions |
| **Planner** | Calendars, goal frameworks, district strategic planning |
| **Coach** | Stakeholder communication, conflict resolution, motivation strategies |
| **Editor** | Drafting correspondence, letters, emails, newsletter articles |
| **Analyst** | Document comparison, policy analysis, cross-referencing sources |
| **Meeting Genie** | Agendas, talking points, meeting preparation |
| **Crisis Helper** | Urgent situations requiring escalation paths and professional contacts |

## How do you know what to trust?

Every policy answer Archie gives includes a confidence signal, so you always know how much weight to put on the response. See [Understanding Archie's answers](/rotary-archie/use/answers/) for the full explanation.

| Signal | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| ✅ Directly supported | A specific RI provision answers this question. Archie cites the exact document and section. |
| ⚠️ Inferred | Related provisions exist but interpretation is required. Archie recommends verification with a specific contact. |
| ❌ Not found | The governing documents do not cover this topic. Archie will not attempt an answer and will route you to RI Support or your Zone coordinator. |

:::caution
For legal, tax, insurance, youth safety, and financial irregularity questions, Archie provides zero substantive guidance. It acknowledges the concern, names the specific professional contacts, and offers to help with related tasks such as drafting communications or pulling policy context.
:::

## What Archie does not do

Archie does not access your club database, make financial decisions, provide legal advice, or replace human judgment. On its own, each conversation starts fresh. Inside a Project, Archie remembers what you tell it; see [Optional: give Archie memory](/rotary-archie/use/memory/). Archie is a tool that makes you more effective; it is not a substitute for the relationships, experience, and discernment that Rotary leadership requires.

## Archie vs. generic AI

| Generic AI (ChatGPT, Claude, etc.) | Archie |
| --- | --- |
| General knowledge, may have outdated or inaccurate Rotary info | Answers from named editions of RI governing documents, listed on the [sources page](/rotary-archie/about/sources/) and checked monthly for updates |
| No citations or source references | Cites specific documents and sections (for example, RI Bylaws §9.030) |
| Generic tone, no awareness of Rotary culture | Rotary-appropriate tone aligned with Service Above Self values |
| Will attempt answers on any topic, including sensitive ones | Hard escalation boundaries on legal, financial, and youth safety matters |
| No structured output format | Consistent format: summary, confidence signal, action steps, references |
