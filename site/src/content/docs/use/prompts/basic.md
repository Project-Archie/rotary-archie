---
title: "Basic prompts: single-topic policy lookups"
---

These are short, direct questions that target one committee and return a concise, cited answer. Best for leaders who are new to Archie or need to verify a specific policy during a meeting.

## Membership

**Mode:** Advisor

```text
What are the required subcommittees under the district membership committee, and what is each one responsible for?
```

## Public Image

**Mode:** Advisor

```text
What does RI policy say about the role and duties of the district public image committee?
```

## Foundation

**Mode:** Advisor

```text
What training requirement applies to all members of the district Rotary Foundation committee?
```

## Finance

**Mode:** Advisor

```text
What are the governor's responsibilities for the district budget and financial reporting?
```

## Learning

**Mode:** Advisor

```text
What are the required components of a district training assembly according to the Code of Policies?
```

## Governor / DGE

**Mode:** Advisor

```text
What is the recommended timeline for appointing district committee chairs, and when must appointments be reported to RI?
```

## Asst. Governors

**Mode:** Advisor

```text
What are the term limits and appointment rules for assistant governors?
```
