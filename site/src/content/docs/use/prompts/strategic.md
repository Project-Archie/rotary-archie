---
title: "Strategic prompts: cross-functional planning"
---

These prompts pull from multiple RI documents, coordinate across committees, and produce comprehensive plans with timelines, metrics, and responsibility assignments. For experienced leaders tackling annual planning, district-wide coordination, or complex governance decisions.

## Membership

**Mode:** Planner + Analyst

```text
Our district lost 4% net membership last year. Analyze the membership committee structure required by RI policy, then build a full-year action plan that assigns quarterly goals to the Attraction, Engagement, and New Club Development subcommittees, with success metrics I can track in Club Central.
```

## Public Image

**Mode:** Planner + Coach

```text
Design a cross-committee visibility strategy that coordinates the Public Image, Membership, and Foundation committees around two major district events (district conference and a community service day). Include a RACI chart, a shared messaging calendar, and talking points aligned with the People of Action campaign.
```

## Foundation

**Mode:** Planner + Analyst

```text
Build a DDF stewardship plan for the Rotary year that aligns Foundation committee goals with the district's priority areas of focus. Include quarterly review checkpoints, a risk register for grant compliance, and a template for the annual DDF usage report to clubs.
```

## Learning

**Mode:** Analyst + Planner

```text
Our PETS attendance was strong but district training assembly attendance dropped 30%. Analyze the required assembly components from RI policy, then propose a restructured format that addresses attendance barriers while keeping us compliant. Include a comparison of in-person, hybrid, and online options with policy implications for each.
```

## Governor / DGE

**Mode:** Planner

```text
Help me build my governor's year in three documents: (1) a district goal framework aligned to the Rotary Citation requirements, (2) a RACI chart mapping every required committee to its key deliverables and deadlines, and (3) a monthly calendar of governor touchpoints (club visits, committee check-ins, reporting deadlines).
```

## Cross-Committee

**Mode:** Planner + Meeting Genie + Editor

```text
Our district conference is in five months. Build a conference planning timeline that coordinates the district conference committee, public image committee, Foundation committee (for recognition awards), and learning committee (for breakout sessions). Include a responsibility matrix and a communications plan with key message drafts.
```

## Finance + Foundation

**Mode:** Analyst + Meeting Genie

```text
The district needs to decide how to allocate this year's DDF across global grants, district grants, and PolioPlus. Build a decision framework that incorporates RI policy requirements, our district's giving history, and a scoring rubric for evaluating competing grant proposals. Include a template agenda for the allocation meeting.
```
